Insight AI

An assistant that knows your school, and only shows each person what they're allowed to see.

01Answers parents and staff 24×7

Fee dues, timetables, circulars, transport timings and policies, answered from your own ERP data and documents on WhatsApp, web and app.

02Role-aware by design

A parent sees only their own child. A teacher sees their classes. The principal sees the whole school.

03Does the paperwork

Reads admission forms, bank receipts and supplier invoices into the ERP, and drafts report-card comments for teachers to approve.

04Spots problems early

Flags likely fee defaults, falling attendance and slipping grades, and sends leadership a weekly summary.

FAQ

Questions schools ask us

Can't find your answer? Email us and we'll reply within one working day.

How long does it take to go live?

Most schools are live within four to six weeks of signing, including data migration and training. Fees and attendance can go live first, with exams and payroll following.

Can you move our data from our current software or Excel?

Yes. We import student, staff, fee and past-result data from Excel or your existing software as part of onboarding.

Is our students' data safe?

Data is hosted on secure cloud servers with daily backups, encrypted connections and role-based access. Every action is logged, and the AI assistant follows the same permissions as the person using it.

Do parents need to install an app?

No. Parents can get notices, fee links and answers on WhatsApp. The parent app is there for those who want it.

Can we start with only a few modules?

Yes. Start with Essentials and upgrade at any time; you only pay the difference for the rest of the year.

Do you work with businesses as well as schools?

Yes. NorthBridge Business ERP runs on the same core for SMEs, institutions and service businesses, and we build custom automations on the tools you already use.
