How it's built

Core → Modules → Workflows → AI → Reports

Each layer builds on the one before it. That's why we can bring a new school or business live in weeks, not quarters.

1 · Core

Core platform

One database, secure APIs and role-based logins for admins, staff, parents and customers.

2 · Modules

Modules

Switch on fees, attendance, inventory or CRM as you need them. Pay for what you use.

3 · Workflows

Workflows

Approvals, escalations and scheduled jobs set up around your existing process.

4 · AI

AI agents

Assistants answer questions from your own policies and records, and agents act on ERP data.

5 · Reports

Reports

PDF and Excel exports, dashboards and AI-written summaries for leadership.

Your data stays in your ERP. AI features work on top of it with the same role permissions as the people using it, so a parent only ever sees their own child's records.
